General Information

Overlooking Front Square, at the heart of Trinity College Dublin’s campus, the Trinity Chapel was designed by Sir William Chambers in 1798 and has been in constant use ever since. The Trinity Chapel is unique among Irish university chapels as it has been reconsecrated (in 1973) for use by all Christian traditions. The entrance is through a Portland stone portico into a beautiful and atmospheric interior, stained-glass windows depicting biblical scenes create a kaleidoscope of colour. Tiered seating on either side of the aisle can accommodate up to 300 people.

Accessibility Highlights

Accessible entrances and circulation routes are available in the building context and surrounding campus paths.

Facilities & Student Services

All entry and exit points in this building have accessible and automated doors to navigate through.The Trinity College Chapel is a Christian Chapel, and only Christian weddings may take place there. The marriage ceremony has to be conducted by an ordained minister of the mainstream Christian Churches. Information about and application to marry in the College Chapel must be made to the Chapel Committee through the Chaplaincy by email only at chaplain@tcd.ie
