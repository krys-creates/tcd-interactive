Regent House

Departments: Front Square examinations, seminar, and event spaces

Hours: Main Archway open 24 hours; during examinations/seminars, interior access is typically 9:00 am-6:00 pm daily

Contact: Trinity College Dublin central switchboard: +353 1 896 1000

General Information

Regent House is a historic Front Square building dating from the early 1700s, with stone construction over three storeys. It has traditionally supported college exam and seminar activity, including use of its upper hall during assessment periods.

Accessibility Highlights

Trinity's physical access guidance states that Regent House is generally accessible, but not fully: the Boydell Room floor is an exception. The central staircase serves upper areas, and the upper main hall is not generally open for unscheduled public access.

Facilities & Student Services

Regent House includes key exam/seminar spaces plus security-support functions in the building. During active exam and seminar windows, operations are managed with controlled opening times and monitored access in the Front Square zone.
